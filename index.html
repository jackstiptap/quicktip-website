02 — What's different

Built around the moment, not the transaction.

Tipping happens in about four seconds of eye contact. Everything below exists to fit inside that window.

01

Proximity, not search

A worker toggles visibility once. Their phone broadcasts over BLE, and they surface in the app of anyone standing nearby. No handles to type, no code to point a camera at — a QR fallback is there for the rare room where Bluetooth won't reach.

02

Fees, split and stated

Moving money costs something, and QuickTip splits that cost rather than pushing it onto one side. The customer covers part at checkout, the worker covers part on the payout. Both shares are shown in the app before anything is confirmed.

03

A record on both sides

Every tip produces an email receipt for the sender and a line item for the worker — status, amount, date. Nothing about a digital tip should be less traceable than a bill in a hand.

03 — For workers

One toggle. Then put the phone away.

Visibility is a single switch on the worker home screen. Flip it on at the start of a shift and you show up to every guest who opens QuickTip nearby — phone in your pocket, hands free.

05 — After the tap

Every tip keeps a paper trail.

Sent tips live in their own tab with amount, date, and status — completed, pending, or failed. Filter to pending when something is still in flight. Nothing disappears into a feed.

06 — Security

Money moves on licensed rails.

Processing

Handled by Square

Card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay payments are processed by Square on PCI DSS-compliant infrastructure.

Storage

No card data on our side

Full card numbers, CVVs, and bank account numbers are never stored on QuickTip servers.

Bluetooth

Nothing retained

BLE data is used for real-time, on-device proximity detection only. Signal data is not collected or kept after the interaction.

Control

Delete on request

Accounts and personal data can be deleted from Settings or by email, subject to records we're required to retain.

Free for customers. Workers pay a small platform fee on tips received, plus a flat fee per cashout. Every applicable fee is shown in the app before a transaction completes.

A worker who enables visibility broadcasts a unique identifier over Bluetooth Low Energy. When a customer opens the app nearby, their device detects those broadcasts and lists the workers around them. No pairing, no QR codes, no typing names — and the worker's phone can stay in a pocket.

Payments are processed by Square. Card details never reach QuickTip servers — they go directly to Square's PCI-compliant infrastructure and come back as a token.

Payments come in through Square, and tips accumulate in an in-app balance. Workers link a PayPal email address to their QuickTip account and cash out to it whenever they want — from PayPal the money can move on to a bank account or Venmo.
